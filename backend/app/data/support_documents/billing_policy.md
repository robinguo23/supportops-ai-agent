# Billing Policy

If a customer believes they were charged twice, they should first check whether one of the charges is pending or temporary.

Duplicate charges may sometimes appear temporarily depending on the payment provider or bank.

If both charges are completed, the customer should provide their order ID, payment date, payment method, and the amount charged.

Support can review billing issues such as duplicate charges, incorrect charges, failed payments, or unexpected payment amounts.

If a duplicate charge is confirmed, support may arrange a refund to the original payment method.
