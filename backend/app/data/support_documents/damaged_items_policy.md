# Damaged Items Policy

If an item arrives damaged, faulty, broken, or defective, the customer should contact support as soon as possible.

The customer should provide their order ID, a short description of the issue, and a photo of the damaged item.

Support may offer a replacement, refund, or return instructions depending on the case.

Customers should not dispose of the damaged item until support has reviewed the issue.

If the damage happened during delivery, the support team may need to contact the delivery carrier.

This policy applies to damaged items, faulty items, broken products, defective products, and items that arrived in poor condition.
