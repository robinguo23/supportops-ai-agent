# Delivery Policy

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 working days.

Express delivery usually takes 1 to 2 working days.

Customers can check their delivery status using their order ID.

If an order has already been dispatched, the delivery address may no longer be changed.

If a parcel is delayed, customers should wait until the estimated delivery date has passed before contacting support.

For late parcels, missing parcels, or delivery status questions, customers should provide their order ID so support can review the delivery information.
