# Refund Request Policy

If a customer wants to request a refund, they should provide their order ID and explain the reason for the refund request.

Common refund request reasons include unwanted items, damaged items, faulty items, wrong items, missing items, duplicate charges, or delayed orders.

In most cases, the customer may need to return the item before the refund can be processed.

Refunds are usually processed within 5 to 7 working days after the returned item has been received and inspected.

If the item is damaged, faulty, or incorrect, the customer should include a short description of the issue and any supporting evidence, such as a photo.

If the customer is unsure whether they are eligible for a refund after reviewing this policy, they may request human support.
