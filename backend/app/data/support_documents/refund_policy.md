# Refund Policy

Refunds are usually processed within 5 to 7 working days after the returned item has been received and inspected.

The refund will normally be issued to the original payment method.

If the refund has not arrived after 10 working days, the customer should contact support with their order ID.

Refunds may be delayed if the returned item is damaged, incomplete, or missing original packaging.

Shipping fees may not always be refundable, depending on the reason for the return.
